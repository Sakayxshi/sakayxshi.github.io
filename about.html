About

Hey there! My name is Omar and I am a Second-year Computer Science student at Constructor University. I build video games,manage Game Development Club in the Univeristy and sometimes write my thoughts on Game Dev in my Blog page right here.

I always loved playing video games, but more than that I wanted to know what is behind them. Why games ask you to play this way and not another. Wether the way you are playing is the way expected. Why a particular moment lands the way it does, and what someone had to decide for it to land at all.

I am currently building my portfolio around game/quest design and game engine tooling (creating mods for all sorts of games). I am also looking for opportunities in the game industry where I can contribute to the craft of creating experiences.
