Like One Who Once Had Wings — Quest Design Document

A quest design document for a Witcher 3 side quest mod. A routine monster contract in a poor Velen village unravels into an investigation of a fellow witcher who has broken his code.

The quest is gated: it only opens into a full side quest if the player finds all four optional investigation pieces during the monster hunt, rewarding attentive play with content inattentive players never see. Five phases, a six-way branching finale, and four distinct world aftermath states.

Written to be implementable — the document includes an NPC casting table with Witcher 3 entity IDs and a per-phase location breakdown across Lindenvale and Novigrad.

TTRPG Forge — Character Creator App

In development

Turning dense tabletop rulebooks into guided character creation. One engine, many rulesets:

  • The Witcher TTRPG
  • Cyberpunk 2020
  • Vampire: The Masquerade
  • Dishonored TTRPG

The Witcher creator is built as a seven-stage wizard — race, lifepath, profession, statistics, pick-up skills, coin and outfit — with rules and options rendered from structured data rather than hardcoded, so new systems plug into the same engine.
